# **TELUS Mobility Mock Call Script – Deceased Customer TOWN Transfer**

**Scenario:** Britney Speer is calling on behalf of her deceased family member, Tracy P Speer-Zz.

**Your role:** TELUS Mobility Customer Service Representative

**Main objective:** Show empathy, explain the TOWN (Transfer of Ownership) process in simple language, make sure Britney understands the 10-day approval window, and confidently guide her through the process without making her feel pressured.

The most important thing in this call is to remember that Britney is grieving. Your approach should be warm, patient, reassuring, and professional. Don't sound like you're rushing to complete a transaction.

I'll structure this as a complete agent-and-customer mock call, including the opening, empathy statements, explanations, probing questions, confirmation, and closing.

## **1. Opening and introduction**

**AGENT:**

"Thank you for calling TELUS Mobility. My name is [Your Name]. How may I assist you today?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"Hi, I'm calling on behalf of my family member, Tracy. She recently passed away, and I was told I need to take over her account."

**AGENT:**

"First of all, I'm truly sorry for your loss. My deepest condolences to you and your family during this difficult time."

"I understand that dealing with an account and these arrangements may be the last thing you want to worry about right now. Please know that I'm here to help make this process as simple and stress-free as possible for you."

"Thank you as well for taking the time to contact us. I'll walk you through everything step by step, and I'll make sure you understand how the process works before we proceed. Is that alright with you?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"Yes, thank you."

**AGENT:**

"Of course. And please feel free to stop me anytime if you have questions or need me to explain anything again. There's absolutely no rush."

## **2. Verification and acknowledgment**

**AGENT:**

"Before we proceed, I understand that you've already completed our standard verification process and that you have the necessary family authority to handle Tracy's account. Thank you for cooperating with us."

"Just to make sure we're on the same page, you're looking to have the existing TELUS Mobility account transferred from Tracy's name into your name. Is that correct?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"Yes, that's correct. I want to take over the account."

**AGENT:**

"Absolutely. I can certainly guide you through that."

## **3. Explain what TOWN means**

This is one of the most important parts of your mock call. Don't immediately say, "I'll process a TOWN for you." Explain what it actually means.

**AGENT:**

"Now, Britney, let me explain what we call a Transfer of Ownership, or TOWN."

"Basically, a Transfer of Ownership means that we're going to move the ownership of Tracy's existing TELUS Mobility account into your name, so you can take responsibility for the account moving forward."

"Think of it as changing the account owner rather than starting everything from scratch. The purpose is to allow you to continue managing the existing mobile service under your own name."

"Now, I want to make sure you understand what will happen throughout this process, so let me break it down into a few simple steps."

## **4. Explain what happens today when the transfer is initiated**

**AGENT:**

"First, since we've already completed the necessary verification and established your authority to handle the account, we'll proceed with initiating the Transfer of Ownership request."

"Once the request is initiated, this does not necessarily mean that the account has already been transferred into your name. It means that we've started the process and that the request will move through the required approval stage."

"After the necessary approval is completed, you'll receive instructions on how to finalize the transfer."

"So, for today, our goal is to get the transfer process started and make sure you know exactly what to expect next."

**CUSTOMER:**

"So does that mean the account will immediately be under my name today?"

**AGENT:**

"That's a great question, Britney. I completely understand why you'd want to know that."

"To clarify, not immediately. Today, we're initiating the transfer request. There is still a final step that needs to be completed before the account officially becomes yours."

"But don't worry. I'll explain that next so you won't be left wondering what happens after today's call."

## **5. Explain the 10-day approval window**

Important part of the scenario

**AGENT:**

"Once the request reaches the approval stage and you receive the notification with the instructions, you'll have a 10-day window to contact TELUS and complete the remaining steps of the transfer."

"That means you'll have 10 days from receiving the approval notification to get back in touch with us."

"During that time, you'll need to contact our team so we can complete the ownership transfer under your name."

"I also want to emphasize that the 10 days are there to give you a window to complete the process. You don't need to panic or feel pressured to do everything immediately."

**CUSTOMER:**

"What if I don't contact TELUS within those 10 days?"

**AGENT:**

"That's completely understandable, Britney, and I'm glad you asked."

"The important thing is that the transfer needs to be completed within that 10-day window. If the window expires before the transfer is completed, the request may no longer be valid, and additional assistance may be needed to proceed."

"That's why I recommend keeping an eye on the notification once you receive it. If you have any concerns or anticipate difficulty completing the process within that time, please contact us as soon as possible so we can guide you on the available next steps."

## **6. Explain how Britney will know when the transfer is complete**

**CUSTOMER:**

"How will I know if the transfer is already completed?"

**AGENT:**

"That's a very good question."

"Once you've completed the remaining steps with our team and the transfer has been successfully finalized, you'll be informed that the account ownership has been transferred."

"You'll then be able to manage the account under your name, subject to the applicable account arrangements."

"I recommend keeping any confirmation or notification you receive for your reference. That way, you'll have something to refer to if you need assistance in the future."

"Does that make sense so far, Britney?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"Yes, it does."

**AGENT:**

"Perfect. I'm glad I could make that clearer for you."

## **7. Explain what Britney needs to do and what she doesn't need to do**

**AGENT:**

"Now, Britney, I also want to make things easier for you by explaining what you actually need to do moving forward."

"For today, since we're initiating the request on our end, you don't need to worry about navigating different systems or figuring out the technical side of the process."

"Your main responsibility will be to watch for the approval notification, follow the instructions provided, and contact TELUS within the 10-day window to complete the transfer."

"You don't need to submit another request today just to start the process we've discussed."

"And if anything is unclear when you receive the notification, you can always reach out to us for assistance. We'll be happy to guide you."

**CUSTOMER:**

"Okay, that's good to know."

**AGENT:**

"Absolutely. I want you to feel comfortable knowing what comes next rather than having to figure everything out on your own."

## **8. Explain possible fees and charges**

This is where you need to be careful. Never promise that the transfer is free unless you've confirmed the applicable bereavement policy.

**AGENT:**

"Now, before we move forward, Britney, I also want to be transparent about any possible fees or charges."

"Generally, a Transfer of Ownership may have an associated service fee. However, since this is a special circumstance involving a deceased account holder, I don't want to give you incorrect information or tell you that you'll be charged something that may not actually apply."

"I would like to make sure that any applicable bereavement-related fee exemption or adjustment is considered before we finalize the transfer."

"Also, if there are any existing device balances, payment obligations, or other outstanding charges associated with the account, those may need to be reviewed as part of the transfer."

"I want you to have a clear understanding of any financial responsibility before you agree to take ownership."

**CUSTOMER:**

"So am I going to pay anything?"

**AGENT:**

"I completely understand your concern, Britney, especially when you're already dealing with so much."

"I don't want to give you a definite amount without confirming the applicable charges on this particular account."

"What I can assure you is that we should identify any applicable fees or remaining financial obligations and explain them to you before you proceed, so there are no unexpected surprises."

## **9. Check for understanding and reassure the customer**

This is a very important quality-assurance checkpoint. Don't just explain everything and immediately move on.

**AGENT:**

"Now, Britney, I know I've shared quite a bit of information with you, so I just want to make sure everything is clear."

"Just to quickly recap:

- Today, we'll initiate the Transfer of Ownership request.
- Once the request reaches the approval stage, you'll receive a notification with further instructions.
- From receiving that notification, you'll have 10 days to contact TELUS and complete the remaining steps.
- Once everything has been successfully completed, the account will officially be under your name.
- Any applicable fees or outstanding financial obligations will be clarified with you before proceeding."

"Is there anything you'd like me to explain again, or do you have any questions or concerns about the process?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"No, I think I understand now."

**AGENT:**

"That's wonderful, Britney. Thank you for taking the time to go through everything with me."

"Before I proceed, I just want to confirm: Are you comfortable with the process I've explained, and would you like us to move forward with initiating the Transfer of Ownership?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"Yes, I'd like to proceed."

**AGENT:**

"Absolutely, Britney. Thank you for confirming. I'll proceed with the transfer request based on the information we've discussed."

## **10. Optional needs-based probing (before any upselling)**

Follow the scenario's success tip

Only do this after Britney understands TOWN and agrees to proceed. Because she's grieving, don't force an offer.

**AGENT:**

"Now, Britney, before we wrap things up, I'd also like to ask if there's anything else related to the mobile service that you'd like assistance with."

"For example, are you planning to continue using the existing mobile number and service, or are there any changes you'd eventually like to make once the account is under your name?"

**CUSTOMER:**

"I just want to keep the number for now."

**AGENT:**

"Absolutely. I understand. Keeping the existing number is important, and we can focus on making the ownership transition as smooth as possible."

"There's no need to make any additional changes today. We can take things one step at a time."

If she expresses interest in a different plan, device, or service, you can explore it gently:

**AGENT:**

"Of course. Once the ownership transfer is completed, I'd be happy to review the available options with you whenever you're ready. There's absolutely no pressure to make any additional decisions today."

## **11. Closing the call with empathy and reassurance**

**AGENT:**

"Before we end our conversation, Britney, I just want to thank you for your patience and for allowing me to assist you today."

"I understand that this isn't an easy situation, and I truly appreciate you taking the time to handle these arrangements for your family."

"Just remember to keep an eye on the approval notification and make sure to contact us within the 10-day period so we can help you complete the remaining steps."

"If you have any questions along the way, please don't hesitate to reach out. We're always here to help."

"Once again, I'm sincerely sorry for your loss. On behalf of TELUS, we wish you and your family strength and comfort during this difficult time."

"Thank you for choosing TELUS Mobility, Britney. My name is [Your Name], and it was my pleasure assisting you today. Please take care and have a wonderful rest of your day."

**CUSTOMER:**

"Thank you for your help."

**AGENT:**

"You're very welcome, Britney. Take care."

# **Additional customer objections and how to handle them**

Your evaluator might throw unexpected questions at you. Here are some possible situations.

### **Scenario A: Customer becomes emotional**

**CUSTOMER:**

"I'm sorry. It's just really difficult because I just lost someone in my family."

**AGENT:**

"Britney, please don't apologize. I completely understand that this is a difficult time for you."

"Take all the time you need. We can go through everything at your own pace. I'm here to help you, and there's no need to feel rushed."

### **Scenario B: Customer doesn't understand TOWN**

**CUSTOMER:**

"I'm still confused. Why can't you just change the name on the account?"

**AGENT:**

"I understand why it might seem like a simple name change, Britney."

"However, a Transfer of Ownership is more than just changing the name. We're officially transferring the responsibility and ownership of the mobile account to another person."

"This ensures that the account is properly established under your name and that you can manage it moving forward."

"Does that explanation make a little more sense?"

### **Scenario C: Customer is worried about losing the number**

**CUSTOMER:**

"Will I lose Tracy's phone number?"

**AGENT:**

"I understand why keeping that number is important to you, Britney."

"The purpose of this process is to transfer ownership of the existing mobile number rather than simply cancel it. We'll follow the appropriate transfer steps to help maintain the number."

"I wouldn't want to promise something without confirming the account's eligibility, but I'll make sure we address that concern before finalizing the transfer."

### **Scenario D: Customer asks whether she needs to visit a store**

**CUSTOMER:**

"Do I need to go to a TELUS store to complete everything?"

**AGENT:**

"That's a fair question. Based on the process we've discussed, your next step is to follow the approval notification and contact our team within the 10-day window."

"If any additional requirements or in-person steps are necessary, we'll make sure those are communicated to you. I don't want you making an unnecessary trip."

### **Scenario E: Customer asks if she will inherit the outstanding device balance**

**CUSTOMER:**

"Will I also have to pay for Tracy's remaining phone payments?"

**AGENT:**

"That's an important question, Britney, and I'm glad you brought it up."

"When ownership is transferred, there may be remaining financial commitments associated with the account, including device payments or other obligations."

"However, I don't want to assume that every existing balance will automatically apply in your situation. We'll need to review the account and explain which responsibilities, if any, would transfer to you before you agree to take ownership."

### **Scenario F: Customer asks if she can think about it first**

**CUSTOMER:**

"Can I think about it first? I'm not sure if I'm ready."

**AGENT:**

"Absolutely, Britney. I completely respect that."

"This is an important decision, and I wouldn't want you to feel pressured into making it."

"My priority today is to make sure you understand the process and have all the information you need. You deserve the time to consider your options."

# **12. Your mock call cheat sheet**

Keep this beside you during your evaluation.

**The 6 things you must accomplish**

1. **Empathy first**

   Express sincere condolences. Acknowledge that the customer is grieving.
2. **Explain TOWN**

   Transfer of Ownership means transferring the existing mobile account ownership and associated responsibilities.
3. **Explain today's process**

   Initiate the request and explain that initiation does not mean the transfer is already finalized.
4. **Explain the 10-day window**

   Britney must contact TELUS within 10 days of receiving the approval notification to complete the process.
5. **Explain fees and responsibilities**

   Clarify applicable fees, possible exemptions, device balances, and other financial obligations.
6. **Confirm understanding and permission**

   Ask if she understands and is comfortable proceeding before initiating the transfer.

## **My biggest advice for your evaluation**

Don't sound like you're reading a script. Make Britney feel that you're talking to her, not just processing an account.

Remember these three things:

1. **Empathy before efficiency.** Don't rush into account procedures immediately after hearing about the death.
2. **Clarity before selling.** Explain every part of TOWN before asking about additional services.
3. **Reassurance before closing.** Make sure Britney knows exactly what happens after the call.

One line I particularly recommend remembering is:

> "Britney, my priority today is to make this process as simple and comfortable as possible for you. We'll take everything one step at a time, and I'll make sure you know exactly what to expect before we proceed."
